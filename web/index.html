About this model

A 33M-parameter decoder-only transformer, trained from scratch on TinyStories (short children's stories) for 450M tokens, on a GTX 1650 Ti laptop GPU in about 21 hours. Final validation loss: 1.37.

Everything runs on your device. The only network request is the one-time download of the model file, which your browser then keeps.
